Delete your account

You can delete your PickMyNest account yourself, at any time, from the app or the web. Nobody has to do it for you and you do not have to ask us.

On the phone

  1. Open PickMyNest and sign in as the owner of the business.
  2. Go to More → Setup → Delete account
  3. Enter your password and type your business name to confirm.
  4. Tap Delete this business, then confirm once more.

On the web

  1. Sign in as the owner.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete this business
  3. Enter your password and type your business name to confirm.
  4. Press Delete this business for good.

Only the owner of a business can do this. A warden cannot delete the account they work in — if you are a warden, ask your owner.

What is deleted

Everything, straight away. There is no grace period and nothing is archived.

Deleting the account removes:

  • Every property, floor, flat, room and bed
  • Every tenant, their stay, their ID photograph and their deposit record
  • Every charge, payment, allocation and electricity bill — the whole ledger
  • Every complaint, expense and mess menu
  • Your own login, and every warden login you created
  • Every signed-in session, on every device

Your subscription is cancelled at the same moment, so your card stops being charged. If we cannot cancel it, the deletion is refused and nothing is removed — we will not leave a card paying for an account that no longer exists. If that happens, try again a few minutes later.

Export anything you need first. The Building screen and every report have an export button, and once the account is gone we cannot recover any of it.

What is kept, and for how long

WhatHow long
Your business data — tenants, ledger, documents, logins Not kept. Removed immediately and irreversibly.
A server log line recording that the account was deleted, with the business name and the owner's email address Up to 30 days, in our server logs, then overwritten. It is how we can answer "what happened to my account" if you ask.
Payment and invoice records held by our payment processor, Stripe Kept by Stripe for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We cannot delete these, and neither can you — they are records of money that changed hands.

We do not sell your data, and we do not keep a copy of it after deletion for any other purpose.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your account and cannot reach the button, email us from the address the account was registered with and ask for it to be deleted. We will confirm who you are before deleting anything.

support@pickmynest.com

If you have simply forgotten your password, use Forgot your password on the sign-in screen instead — a reset link will reach you by email, and then you can delete the account yourself.

Deleting your account is not the same as cancelling your subscription. If you only want to stop paying and keep your records, open Subscription and cancel the plan instead — your data stays where it is.
